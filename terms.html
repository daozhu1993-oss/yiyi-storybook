Using StoryComet

Effective 10 September 2026

StoryComet is an interactive picture-book reading experience operated by DESIGNCODE IO PTE. LTD.. You can read the first three books without an account. Optional accounts are intended for parents and other adults.

Reading and accounts

You may use the app for personal reading and learning. Use an email or Google account that belongs to you, keep sign-in codes private, and sign out on shared devices. Signed-in parents can save reading progress and reader profiles to their account.

Membership purchases

The early-bird price is US$39 per year for a yearly subscription or US$79 for lifetime membership. Standard prices are US$79 per year and US$149 respectively. Yearly membership renews automatically at US$39 per year, plus applicable tax, until cancelled. Cancel in My account → Manage billing before the next renewal to avoid another charge. Cancellation preserves access through the end of the paid period. Upgrading to lifetime stops future yearly renewals after the lifetime payment is confirmed; it does not automatically refund the current yearly period. Existing one-time annual purchases keep their original 12-month term and do not become subscriptions. Lifetime is a single payment with no recurring charge. Lifetime membership lasts for as long as the StoryComet service operates; it does not guarantee that the service or any particular book will remain available forever.

Purchases are for adults and are linked to the parent account used before checkout. Prices exclude applicable tax. The final tax and any local-currency conversion are shown before you pay. Payments marked “Sold through Link” use Stripe Managed Payments, with Link acting as merchant of record and providing payment receipts, order management and transaction support. Membership is activated only after payment is confirmed. Contact hello@storycomet.app for purchase or refund questions. Nothing in these terms limits mandatory consumer rights.

Respect the service and its content

Do not attempt to access another person’s account, interfere with the service, or upload harmful material. Books, artwork, narration and software may be subject to copyright and other rights. Access to the app does not transfer ownership or grant permission to republish or resell its content. Third-party software and fonts remain subject to their applicable licenses; license notices are included with the app.

Availability and changes

Features and available books may change, and the service may be interrupted for maintenance or technical problems. The app is a reading aid, not a guarantee of a particular educational outcome. Nothing on this page limits rights that applicable law does not allow to be limited.

Contact

For support or account deletion, email hello@storycomet.app. Our privacy page explains account information and the services the app uses.
