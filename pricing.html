A few things to know

Will I be charged again?

Yearly membership renews automatically at US$39 per year, plus applicable tax, until you cancel in My account → Manage billing. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of your paid period. Lifetime is one payment with no recurring charge. Earlier one-time annual purchases keep their original terms.

What does lifetime mean?

Lifetime membership lasts for as long as the StoryComet service operates. It is tied to your parent account. Books and features may change over time.

How do I find my purchase?

Sign in with the same parent account you used before checkout. You can see your membership and its expiry in My account. If a payment is still processing, we will show that clearly.

Need help with a payment?

Email hello@storycomet.app from your parent account’s email address. Please do not email card details.
